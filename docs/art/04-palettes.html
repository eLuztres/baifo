Baifo art 4/5: color palettes

Three palettes for light and dark, shown on the real home and listing pages (screenshots of the running shop with only the theme tokens swapped). All three keep the teal of the logo, use the island colors from the moodboard (sea turquoise, dune sand, volcanic red-brown, whitewashed houses) and pass WCAG AA for text and buttons. Pick one; it's applied only through BRAND_COLOR_* / BRAND_DARK_COLOR_* in .env. Click a screenshot to see it full size.
