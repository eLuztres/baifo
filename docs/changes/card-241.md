# Card #241: Baifo marketing plan (video-first)

## What changed

- New `docs/marketing/baifo-marketing-plan.md`: the facts every post must match, the five audience groups, the main
  promise ("Mándanos una foto por WhatsApp. Nosotros hacemos el anuncio."), channels with weekly effort, content
  pillars with Higgsfield prompts, the posting rhythm, launch ideas and what we measure.
- New `docs/marketing/calendar-4-weeks.md`: a setup checklist and four weeks day by day, with posts, flyer and
  Facebook group texts ready to copy (Spanish, English for expat groups).
- Documents only, no code change.

## Why

More listings by WhatsApp and El Corral, and more visits to baifo.es, with a plan one person can keep up.

## Review notes

- Facts checked against `docs/MARKETPLACE.md` and the legal texts: 5 € / 10 € big, Bizum or cash, 3 months, five
  languages, `vendido` command, `/explore?hoy`, the seller decides if the number shows, no analytics cookies.
- Fixed: the plan counted channels by UTM, but the site has no analytics to read them. The prefilled WhatsApp text is
  now the source count; UTMs are kept as optional for when analytics is added.

## How to check by hand

1. Read both files; change the week-4 targets, the handle `@baifo.es` and the print run to your own numbers.
2. Decide on "first 30 listings free" (week 2). A 0 € "free" option in the admin would be a separate card, because
   **Pagado** books a fee.
3. Do the "Before day 1" checklist, then start week 1 on a Monday.
