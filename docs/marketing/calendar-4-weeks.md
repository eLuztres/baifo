# Baifo: first 4 weeks

Goes with [baifo-marketing-plan.md](baifo-marketing-plan.md). Copy is ready to paste. Spanish first, English under it for expat groups.
`[WA]` = the WhatsApp link with a prefilled text per channel (see plan, section 4). `[link]` = baifo.es with the channel's UTM.
Every Reel ends with the goat sign-off and "Mándalo por WhatsApp · link en la bio".

## Before day 1 (setup, ~3 h)

- [ ] Instagram + TikTok handle `@baifo.es` (or closest free), bio, link to `[link]` and `[WA]`.
- [ ] Facebook page, Google Business Profile for El Corral (address, hours, 5 photos).
- [ ] Search Console: sitemap sent, property verified.
- [ ] WhatsApp Business: quick replies from `docs/whatsapp-quick-replies.md`, a WhatsApp channel "Baifo · nuevos anuncios".
- [ ] Sheet with Facebook groups (name, members, rules, allowed day) and the Friday review tab.
- [ ] Higgsfield: goat intro, goat sign-off, 3 island b-roll clips (prompts in the plan).
- [ ] Flyer A5 + card A6 with QR to `[WA]` (source `flyer`). Print 100 / 300.
- [ ] Ask 5 friends or neighbours to send a real listing, so the feed isn't empty on day 1.

FAQ carousel answers (ES):
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** 5 € por anuncio (10 € cosas grandes), 3 meses online.
- **¿Cómo pago?** Bizum o en efectivo en El Corral.
- **¿Quién me escribe?** Quien quiera comprar te escribe directamente. Tú decides si se muestra tu número.
- **¿Y si lo vendo?** Escribe "vendido" en el chat y lo quitamos.
