# Baifo marketing plan (video-first)

Goal for the first 3 months: **more listings sent by WhatsApp** and **more people looking at baifo.es**.
The 4-week start is in [calendar-4-weeks.md](calendar-4-weeks.md).

## 1. What we sell (the facts every post must match)

From `docs/MARKETPLACE.md` and the site texts. Never promise more than this.

- You send a photo or a few words by WhatsApp. No account, no password.
- We make the photo look good and write the listing in five languages. A person checks it before it goes out.
- It costs 5 € (10 € for big things), paid by Bizum or in cash at El Corral. It stays online up to 3 months.
- Buyers write to the seller directly. Baifo takes nothing from the sale.
- Four kinds of listing: something for sale, something you're looking for (Busco), a service or class, an event.
- El Corral is a real place: drop off or pick up what you sold or bought, get help with your listing, meet neighbours.

**Wording rule for "2 minutes":** sending takes 2 minutes; going online needs our review and the payment.
So say "Mándalo en 2 minutos", never "online en 2 minutos".

## 2. Audience

One island, five groups. The bottleneck is **sellers** (no listings, nothing to look at), so the first month aims at them.

| Group | What they want done | Where they are | What holds them back | Hook that answers it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Locals (Majoreros, long-time residents) | Sell the bike, the sofa, the kids' clothes without hassle | Facebook groups, WhatsApp, Instagram | "Wallapop is full of strangers and haggling"; distrust of new apps | Neighbours, WhatsApp they already use, a real place to hand things over |
| Expats and long-stay residents | Furnish a flat, sell before moving away, find a service in English | Expat Facebook groups, Instagram | Language, not knowing who to trust | Listing written in their language; "moving away? send it all in one chat" |
| Tourists and digital nomads | Rent or buy surf gear, find a class, an event tonight | Instagram, TikTok, Google | Short stay, need it today | Events "Hoy" and classes; Reels of island life |
| Small producers and makers (cheese, aloe, crafts, plants) | Be found without running a web shop | Markets, Instagram | No time for social media and photos | We make the photo and the text; you just send it |
| Services (surf school, yoga, handyman, cleaning, lessons) | A steady trickle of new clients | Facebook groups, Google Maps | "Another platform to keep up" | One listing, 3 months, people write to you directly |

## 3. Message and promise

**Core promise (Spanish, main):** *Mándanos una foto por WhatsApp. Nosotros hacemos el anuncio.*
**Short line for videos and flyers:** *Vende en Fuerteventura con un WhatsApp.*
**Identity line (already on the site):** *Hecho en la isla, para la isla.*

Proof points, in this order (each one answers a doubt):
1. **Easy:** one WhatsApp chat, no account (answers "another app?").
2. **It looks good:** before/after of the photo (answers "my photos are bad").
3. **Real people:** we check every listing; El Corral is a place you can walk into (answers "is this a scam?").
4. **Cheap and clear:** 5 € for 3 months, nothing on the sale (answers "what's the catch?").

Tone: neighbourly, plain, a bit of humour with the goat (baifo = young goat). Short sentences. Tú, not usted.
No exclamation marks, no "revolucionario", no "la mejor plataforma".

Psychology used, and how:
- **Activation energy / Fogg:** the ask is always one small step ("manda una foto"), with the WhatsApp link right there.
- **Before/after contrast:** the AI photo next to the original is the strongest visual we have.
- **Social proof + unity:** real neighbours, real town names (Corralejo, Puerto, Morro Jable). Only show sellers who said yes.
- **Mere exposure / rule of 7:** the same goat, colours and line everywhere, many small touches instead of one big launch.
- **Reciprocity:** free help at El Corral (photo, text, price) before anyone pays.
- **Scarcity, only if true:** "los primeros 30 anuncios gratis" has a real limit; we count and stop.

## 4. Channels

| Instagram | Main stage: show listings and the island | 3 Reels (9:16), 2 feed posts (3:4 carousel), Stories daily | 3–4 h |

Facebook groups to start with (check rules before posting, some ban ads or allow them on one weekday only):
Fuerteventura buy and sell groups, "Compra venta Fuerteventura", "Corralejo community", expat groups (English, German, Italian).
Keep a list with name, members, rules and posting day in a sheet.

WhatsApp links prefill a text per channel (e.g. `wa.me/<number>?text=Hola%20Baifo%20(flyer)`), so the first message
tells us where they came from. This is our main source count. Site links can carry a UTM
(`https://baifo.es/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio`, `flyer`, `facebook`, `tiktok`), but the site has no analytics,
so nothing reads them yet. They only pay off if web analytics is added later (its own card).

### Reel recipe (every video)

1. 0–2 s: hook on screen in big text (a question or the price).
2. 2–8 s: the thing (before/after, the chat, the person).
3. Last 2 s: goat + "Mándalo por WhatsApp" + link in bio.
4. Spanish on-screen text, subtitles in English. Caption: 1–2 lines, a town name, 3–5 local hashtags
   (`#fuerteventura #corralejo #puertodelrosario #canarias #segundamano`).

## 6. Rhythm

What one person can keep up, about 6–7 hours a week:

- **Mon:** batch day. Pick 5 listings, cut 3 Reels, write captions, plan the week.
- **Tue / Thu / Sat:** Reel (Instagram + TikTok + Facebook page), 19:00–20:30.
- **Wed / Sun:** 3:4 carousel on Instagram.
- **Daily:** 2–3 Stories + WhatsApp Status with the newest listings (10 min).
- **Twice a week:** one post in a Facebook group (rotate groups, never the same group twice a week).
- **Fri:** 10-minute review of the numbers (section 8).

## 7. Launch ideas

| Idea | What it is | Cost | Risk / how to handle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First 30 listings free** | Code word "BAIFO30" in the WhatsApp chat. Tapping Pagado would book a 5 € fee that never came in, so a 0 € "free" option in the admin is a small card first | 30 × 5 € = 150 € not earned | People expect free forever. Say the number, count down in Stories, stop at 30 |
| **Listing of the week** | Every Friday we pick one listing and give it a Reel and a group post | Time only | None; sellers share it themselves |
| **Local partner** | A café, surf shop or market stall in Corralejo with the flyer and QR at the till; their own listing free | Flyers + one listing | Pick one with foot traffic and a good name |
| **"Moving away" pack** | For expats leaving: send everything in one chat, one price for up to 5 items | Decide the price first | Needs a rule in the admin; start manually |
| **El Corral open afternoon** | Bring your things, we take the photos and make the listings with you | An afternoon | Good Reel and seller-story material |
| **Flyers** | A5 at El Corral, A6 on noticeboards | ~40 € print | Use the `flyer` prefilled WhatsApp text to see if it works |

Start with **listing of the week**, **flyers** and **local partner** (cheap, low risk).
Run the **free first 30** in week 2 only if the chef agrees: it is the strongest push, but it costs the first income.

## 8. What we measure

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| New WhatsApp chats | WhatsApp Business statistics; prefilled text shows the source | 30 / week |

| Visits | Vercel dashboard requests (rough, no analytics on the site) + Search Console clicks | 500 / week |
| Followers and Reel views | Instagram / TikTok insights | Watch the trend, not the number |
| Best channel | Prefilled WhatsApp text of each chat | One clear winner by week 4 |

**North star:** listings live per week. Everything else explains it.

Friday review (10 minutes, one row per week in a sheet):
week · chats · listings sent · listings live · visits · best Reel (views) · best channel · one thing to change.

## 9. Things to avoid

- Spam in Facebook groups (one post per group per week, read the rules, answer comments).
- Fake sellers, fake numbers, AI-made "customers".
- Promising "online in 2 minutes", "free" without a limit, or that Baifo checks the products themselves.
- Showing a seller's face, name or number without their OK.
